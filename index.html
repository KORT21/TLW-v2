The Story

a tiny house built to make you slow down

The Little Wanderer is a beautifully crafted tiny house perched above the shores of Lake Wakatipu in the quiet neighbourhood of Sunshine Bay — just five minutes from Queenstown, yet a world away from the crowds. Designed for couples and small groups who want to unwind without missing out, it's a place where thoughtful design meets genuine wildness.

Wake up to Cecil Peak rising from the water, soak under the stars in your private wood-fired hot tub, and spend your evenings around the wood burner with a glass of Central Otago red. Two bedrooms, a fully equipped kitchen, open-plan living, and your own private outdoor terrace. Everything you need. Nothing you don't.
